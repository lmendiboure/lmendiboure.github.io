IoT Systems Design · Course map

Design connected systems,
one decision at a time.

This course is organised as four missions. You will build, explain, challenge and revise IoT designs rather than memorise a catalogue of technologies.

Course rule: make the need, assumptions and constraints explicit before defending a technology.

Each mission reuses the same design habit: produce → compare → challenge → formalise → transfer → revise.
By the end of the course

Four capabilities, not four technology lists.

1Model the systemTranslate a physical-world need into functions, components and information flows.
2Defend communicationCompare communication choices from explicit requirements and infrastructure assumptions.
3Place computationReason about device, edge and cloud roles instead of treating “the cloud” as a default.
4Challenge the designRevise an architecture when scale, failures, mobility or dependencies change.
