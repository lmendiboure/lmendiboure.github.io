Activity 4Discover communication patterns
Campus missionYour flows now need communication paths.Do not choose a technology yet. First understand the shapes of networks that could carry those flows.

Start from the communication problem — not from a standard name.

You are not expected to know Wi-Fi, Bluetooth LE, IEEE 802.15.4, LoRaWAN or NB-IoT yet. First learn the shape of the network each family is trying to build.

Before naming a technologyAsk four concrete questions

▥How much data?Tiny messages, regular sensing, images?
ϟWhat can the device afford?Tight battery, normal battery, mains power?
⌂What infrastructure exists?Phone, local network, gateway, operator coverage, none?
