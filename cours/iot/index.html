Your role

You own the system-level decisions.

Campus operations sets the outcomes. Field, network and platform teams bring constraints, evidence and incidents. Your responsibility is to turn that information into a connected system that can actually work — and to explain why your design still makes sense when the situation changes.

Your job is not to guess the “best” technology.Start from the need, make assumptions explicit, then defend the architecture that follows.
