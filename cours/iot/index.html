Activity 6Choose under constraints

Which candidate is the best fit given these assumptions?

There may be several feasible solutions. Choose one, commit your answer, then reveal the reasoning. Afterwards, change one assumption and ask whether your decision survives.

Important:“Best” only means best among these candidates under the stated requirements.Change one assumption and the answer may change.
