# Session 1 — pedagogy notes

The student site is intentionally designed around a persistent case plus transfer cases rather than a technology catalogue.

## Design choices

- **Persistent concrete case:** the campus environmental-monitoring mission stays visible from architecture through stress-testing. Case-based teaching is useful when the situation is realistic, contextual, decision-oriented, and does not collapse to one obvious answer.
- **Transfer cases:** wearable, rural sensing, camera, metering and local constrained-device cases deliberately vary surface features so students must reuse underlying decision principles rather than memorize “the campus solution”.
- **Scaffolding for novices:** technical names are introduced after plain-language network shapes. Optional vocabulary layers reduce the amount of jargon that must be processed at first contact.
- **Active/interactive work:** students classify, construct, compare, commit, explain and revise. The design favours constructive and interactive engagement rather than passive reading.
- **Retrieval practice:** the Technology Detective retrieves concepts immediately after guided discovery, and the final four-question Memory Lock asks students to retrieve the session structure before seeing the synthesis.
- **Game elements:** commitment, uncertainty, progressive clues, changed assumptions and expert rounds provide challenge and feedback. Points/rankings are intentionally secondary because the goal is reasoning, not competition.
