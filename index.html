Léo Mendiboure

Associate Professor in Computer Science

At Université de Pau et des Pays de l'Adour, I lead the CPSA team of the LIUPPA laboratory. I study distributed infrastructures shared by several parties: how to orchestrate their resources across the edge-cloud continuum, how to design programmable networks that connect them, and how to trust the computations they run. Railways and connected mobility are my main application domains.

Ph.D. in Computer Science, University of Bordeaux (2020). Previously tenured researcher at Université Gustave Eiffel.

Research

Three research axes on shared distributed infrastructures, each combining models and algorithms with experimentation.

Resource orchestration in the edge-cloud continuum

Allocating computing and network resources when several operators share the same infrastructure: service placement in edge federations, task offloading, sizing of edge sites. I design mechanisms, from auctions to multi-agent reinforcement learning, that meet application requirements while remaining fair to each party, with energy and carbon as additional constraints.

Programmable network architectures

Software-defined networks, slicing and multi-connectivity to let several services and technologies share one network: SDN control architectures, slicing for FRMCS, hybrid 5G and satellite links. This work relies on emulators and testbeds that I develop with my colleagues, such as Emu5GNet.

Trust in distributed systems

Making it possible to trust participants that one does not control, from blockchain-based authentication and access control to verifiable and confidential computation today. I combine zero-knowledge proofs, trusted execution environments and secret sharing, with federated learning as the main use case.
